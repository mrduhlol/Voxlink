A short path
from phone to PC.

Keep both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Voxlink sends audio directly across your local network.

Local network use: audio is not encrypted or authenticated. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network and do not expose UDP port 4950 to the internet.

  1. 01Install the Android senderDownload the APK and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.
  2. 02Set up your Linux computerChoose the desktop app for a virtual microphone, or the receiver if you want a WAV recording.
  3. 03Connect over Wi-FiEnter your computer's local IP address in the phone app, use port 4950, then start the listener and sender.

Voxlink is open source and free to use.
